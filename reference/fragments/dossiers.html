« Forty years of programmes, four thousand concerts, one question: what does a season look like? »

01 — THE BRIEF

THE HALL WANTED ITS music TO BE SEEN BEFORE IT WAS HEARD.

Their archive held every programme since 1982 — composers, keys, durations, applause lengths. Data nobody had ever looked at twice. Our brief: turn that archive into the visual identity of the coming season.

LISTENING WITH YOUR EYES
02 — THE SYSTEM

A TOUCHDESIGNER NETWORK THAT composes LIKE THE ORCHESTRA.

Audio analysis drives geometry: key signatures choose palettes, tempo bends the grid, applause length decides how long a frame survives. 40,000 generated frames — curated by hand, red marker on printouts, taste over stamina.

03 — THE OUTPUT

EVERY NIGHT'S POSTER IS played, NOT DESIGNED.

The season launched with posters, tickets and façade projections all rendered from the same system — each night unique, all nights unmistakably one voice. The identity now belongs to the hall's own team: they play it, we tuned it.
